VSYNTH represents a fusion of traditional violin performance techniques with modern digital synthesis technology. Built around an Arduino microcontroller platform, the system captures bow dynamics, string positions, and performance gestures to generate expressive synthesized audio in real-time.

The project integrates multiple sensor systems including pressure-sensitive bow detection, capacitive string position sensing, and accelerometer-based gesture recognition. These inputs drive a sophisticated synthesis engine capable of producing both realistic violin tones and experimental electronic sounds.

Key features include real-time audio processing, MIDI compatibility, and customizable sound banks that allow performers to explore new sonic territories while maintaining the intuitive feel of traditional violin technique. The system bridges the gap between acoustic and electronic music performance, opening new possibilities for contemporary musical expression.
