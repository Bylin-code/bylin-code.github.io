At Formlabs in Somerville, Massachusetts, I worked on imaging hardware and mechanical testing for a next-generation SLA 3D printer.

- **Camera and lighting:** owned development of an embedded GMSL camera and programmable lighting system, from Onshape chassis design and KiCad PCB layouts to electromechanical integration, DFM, and C camera-driver firmware.
- **Automated imaging:** built a rig that captured 2,000+ images across 10 PWM lighting configurations, seven lenses, and four sensors to guide optics selection, plus a local dashboard for live tuning.
- **Mechanical testing:** designed force-sensing fixtures to measure resin-mixing arm deflection, with C++ data logging and one-click Excel reports to inform geometry and material choices.

The product I worked on has not yet been released, so I’m keeping the details limited for now.
