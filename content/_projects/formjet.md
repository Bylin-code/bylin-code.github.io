**FORMJET** is an automated wash-and-cure station my team and I built in 48 hours at a hackathon. I designed the electronics and wrote the embedded firmware, while supporting CAD, mechanical design, and system integration.

I built a pump-and-valve system that switches between washing solution and clean water. A moving nozzle and rotating platform rinse the print from multiple angles, then UV LEDs cure it as it spins. My firmware coordinates the pump, valves, stepper motors, and LEDs into a single wash-and-cure cycle.
