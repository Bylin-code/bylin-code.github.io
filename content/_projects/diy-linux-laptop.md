I put a Raspberry Pi, monitor, and keyboard into a styrofoam box to make my own Linux laptop. For storage, I salvaged an SSD from a broken ThinkPad my dad had and configured the Pi to boot from it.

It was a fun assembly project and my introduction to experimenting with Linux: fitting the parts together, figuring out the boot setup, and exploring an operating system I could tinker with.
