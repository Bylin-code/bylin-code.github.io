I built a video game controller on breadboards using an Arduino Pro Micro, chosen for its native USB support. I wired up buttons and joysticks, programmed their inputs, and used it to play Minecraft on my computer.

Writing the controls myself let me add custom hotkeys for actions beyond a standard controller’s bindings. It was a hands-on introduction to turning physical inputs into something I could use in a game.
